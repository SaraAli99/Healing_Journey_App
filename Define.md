
# Phase 02: Define (Strategic Direction) 🎯

In this phase, I synthesized all the raw data gathered during the Empathize stage to define the core problem and structure the solution for **Healio Mind**.

---

## 1. User Personas 👤
Based on my research, I identified **Nadia** as our primary persona.
* **Profile:** Nadia is a highly stressed surgeon (Medical Professional) from Kuwait.
* **Pain Points:** Works 60+ hours a week, feels overwhelmed, and distrusts traditional clinical solutions.
* **Goals:** Needs a deep, professional escape to heal emotionally and disconnect from daily pressure in a private, non-judgmental environment.

---

## 2. Empathy Mapping & POV 🧠
I analyzed what our users Think, Say, Do, and Feel.
* **POV Statement:** "Nadia needs a safe, holistic, and meaningful recovery experience because she is burned out and needs to reconnect with herself away from the stigma of her professional circle."

---

## 3. User Journey Map (UJM) 🗺️
I mapped Nadia’s experience across 5 key stages: **Awareness, Consideration, Booking, Service, and Aftercare.**

* **Key Insights from the Map:**
    * **Awareness Stage:** Users feel confused and exhausted while searching for "Burnout recovery."
    * **Booking Stage:** A major pain point is the "Fear of hidden costs" and complex logistics.
    * **Opportunity:** Provide 100% transparent price breakdowns and all-inclusive packages to reduce anxiety.

---

## 4. HMW Questions (How Might We?) 💡
To bridge the gap between the problem and the solution, I formulated several HMW questions:
* **HMW** make the booking process feel as calm as the healing journey itself?
* **HMW** verify the credibility of international experts to build 100% trust?
* **HMW** ensure total privacy for high-profile professionals like Nadia?
