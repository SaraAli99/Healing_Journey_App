# Phase 04: Design System (Visual Identity) 🎨 💎

To ensure consistency and scalability for **Healio Mind**, I developed a comprehensive Design System. This system acts as a single source of truth for all visual elements, ensuring a harmonious user experience.

---

## 1. Logo & Symbolism 🏷️
* **Concept:** The logo combines elements of "Growth" and "Peace" (The Lotus/Mind symbol).
* **Usage:** Defined clear spacing and variations for different backgrounds to maintain brand integrity.

---

## 2. Color Palette (The Healing Colors) 🎨
I chose colors that evoke trust, serenity, and professional care:
* **Primary Green:** Represents growth, healing, and nature.
* **Neutral Grays:** Used for text and backgrounds to provide a clean, modern feel.
* **Feedback Colors:** Defined colors for Success (Green), Warning (Orange), and Error (Red) to guide the user.

---

## 3. Typography ✍️
* **Typeface:** Selected a clean, highly readable font (IBM Plex Sans Arabic/English) to ensure accessibility for all users.
* **Hierarchy:** Established clear scales for Headings (H1, H2, H3), Body Text, and Captions.

---

## 4. Components Library ⚙️
I built a library of reusable components to speed up the design and development process:
* **Buttons:** Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary styles with Hover, Active, and Disabled states.
* **Inputs & Forms:** Clean input fields with clear labels and error validation states.
* **Icons:** A custom icon set that is consistent in stroke weight and style.
* **Navigation & Tabs:** Mobile-optimized bottom navigation and top tab bars.

---

## 5. Grid & Spacing 📐
* **Grid System:** Used a standard 8pt grid system to maintain perfect alignment and vertical rhythm across all screens.
* **Responsive Layout:** Designed with a mobile-first approach to ensure the app looks great on all device sizes.
