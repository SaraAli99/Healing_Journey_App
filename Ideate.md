# Phase 03: Ideate (Solution Architecture) 💡 🏗️

In this phase, I translated the strategic goals from the Define stage into a structured functional system.

---

## 1. Mind Mapping (The Starting Point) 🧠
Before structuring the app, I created a **Mind Map** to brainstorm the entire **Healio Mind** ecosystem. This helped me visualize:
* **The Core Experience:** Linking travel destinations with specific mental health treatments.
* **Support Systems:** How the community, experts, and booking engine interconnect.
* **User Emotional States:** Ensuring the app provides "Calm" and "Safety" at every branch of the map.

---

## 2. Feature Prioritization 📋
Based on the Mind Map, I extracted and categorized the essential features:
* **Core:** Smart Search, Program Details, Secure Booking.
* **Social:** Success Stories, Community Forums.
* **Professional:** Expert Consultation (Video/Chat), Medical Data Privacy.

---

## 3. Card Sorting 🗂️
To ensure intuitive navigation, I organized the brainstormed features into 6 main groups:
* **Group 2:** Home & Overview.
* **Group 3:** Discovery & Explore.
* **Group 4:** Programs & Bookings.
* **Group 5:** Community & Support.
* **Group 6:** Personal Profile.

---

## 4. Information Architecture (SiteMap) 🗺️
I developed a **Sitemap** to define the app's hierarchy, ensuring a flat structure that minimizes the user's effort to find information.

---

## 5. User Flow Maps 🔄
Finally, I mapped out the detailed **User Flows** to handle the logic for:
* **Booking Journey:** (Selection -> Decision -> Payment -> Confirmation).
* **Consultation Logic:** Connecting users with verified professionals.
