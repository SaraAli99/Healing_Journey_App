# Phase 05: Wireframing (The Blueprint) 📐 📱

After establishing the Information Architecture and Design System, I moved to the **Wireframing** phase. The goal here was to focus on the layout, hierarchy, and functionality without being distracted by colors or images.

---

## 1. Onboarding & Assessment 📝
* **Questionnaires:** Designed a clean, step-by-step onboarding flow to understand the user's mental state and goals.
* **Logic:** Ensuring the questions are asked one at a time to avoid overwhelming users like "Nadia."

---

## 2. Discovery & Home 🔍
* **Home - Find Program:** A structured dashboard where users can quickly search for programs, filter by category, and see featured healing destinations.
* **Visual Hierarchy:** Large, clear cards to emphasize destination choices.

---

## 3. Community & Peer Support 👥
* **Community Feed:** A dedicated space for users to share stories and tips anonymously. 
* **Layout:** Designed for high readability and easy engagement (Likes/Comments).

---

## 4. Consultation & Booking Flow 📅
* **Select Therapist:** A specialized interface to browse verified experts, view their specialties, and book a session.
* **Booking Steps:** A linear, multi-step process for program booking to ensure clarity on costs and dates.

---

## 5. Iterative Testing 🔄
These wireframes were built and refined to ensure:
* **Minimal Friction:** Reducing the number of steps to complete a booking.
* **Clarity:** Every button and icon has a clear purpose.
* **Accessibility:** Large touch targets and readable text sizes.
